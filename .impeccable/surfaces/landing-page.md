## Direction contract

THESIS: The offer proves itself before it's stated — visitors meet the actual hazard legend before any sales copy, because a citation-first product should demonstrate specificity immediately, not promise it.

OWN-WORLD: Inherits the Document Review workspace's settled system verbatim — warm parchment ground (#F3EEE2), ink-brown contour rule (#3B362C), single hazard red accent (#C0202B) reserved for danger/CTA emphasis, Overpass for headings/legend, system-ui stack for body prose, JetBrains Mono for data/counts. No new palette or type introduced for this surface.

STORY: A freelancer arrives skeptical of another "AI legal tool." The hazard legend proves specificity in seconds — these are the actual seven things checked, not a vague promise. A dense grid of small real-shaped incident cards proves this isn't hypothetical. A wayfinding-style progressive path (one message per panel: what we found, how it's proven, what you do next) carries them to upload with no ambiguity about the next step.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width hero rendered as the hazard legend itself — seven legend entries (icon, severity-tier color chip, clause name) arranged as a real trail-map key, a headline stating the citation guarantee in one line, a single "Start your route" CTA styled as a stamped trailhead marker.

## Scope

Mode: Persuade. Surface: marketing landing page — the pre-signup, first-time-visitor page making the case to try Redline.

Audience/job: A freelancer who has seen "AI legal tool" claims before and is skeptical by default. Job: decide, within seconds, whether this one is different, and upload a document if so.

Content/constraints: Real research-derived incidents (a freelance illustrator's non-compete enforced years later, a Forbes freelancer's indemnification exposure, FTC auto-renewal actions, HRW's gig-worker survey) may appear as the evidence behind Redline's own design decisions, but must never be dressed up as Redline's own customer stories — no real Redline testimonials exist yet (see PRODUCT.md, Evidence on Hand). Any incident-grid content must be framed as "why we built this," not fabricated customer proof. No pricing claims (pricing is explicitly undecided in PRODUCT.md).
