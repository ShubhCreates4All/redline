Why these seven

RESEARCH RECORD

Each clause type earned its place on the key from a documented case, not from a list of things that sound alarming. Where the evidence was thin, that is stated too.

A non-compete from a brief freelance engagement was pressed four years later, and creators turned down future work rather than test it.

Non-compete Illustrator's own published account

A freelance journalist funded her own legal defense because the contract made her responsible for the client's exposure, not just her own work.

Indemnification Reported, Forbes contributor case

A business debit card was charged for nine years, more than $2,500 in total, after the subscriber believed the account had been cancelled.

Auto-renewal FTC enforcement record

Of 127 Texas gig workers surveyed, 40 had been deactivated at least once; nearly half of those were later cleared of what triggered it.

Unilateral termination Human Rights Watch, 2025

A flat-fee contract review by an attorney averages $390–400, which is the honest alternative this is measured against.

Cost context ContractsCounsel, 34 projects

Liability caps and fee escalators have no documented dispute behind them, only law-firm explainers. They are checked anyway, and ranked lower because of it.

Stated weak evidence Redline research, gap noted

None of these are Redline customers, and none are presented as proof that Redline works. They are the record that decided what it looks for and how severely it ranks each find.

What it will not do

STATED LIMITS

A tool that claims everything is worth nothing to a skeptic. These are refusals, not a roadmap.

Read scanned documents

Optical character recognition misreads words. A quoted sentence taken from misread text is a citation to something the document does not say, which breaks the one guarantee this product makes.

Replace a lawyer

This is built to beat signing unread and to beat a cheap one-off review. It is not built to carry a high-stakes negotiation on its own.

Ask where you live

Enforceability of a non-compete swings by state. Redline names that on the flag and tells you to verify locally, rather than collecting your jurisdiction and guessing.
